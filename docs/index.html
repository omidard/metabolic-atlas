      Is a found pathway flux-feasible in the chosen GEM on a medium, and how much flux does it carry?

      Three solvers per pathway, all in this browser (GLPK-WASM): a feasibility LP (product export maximised while every step carries flux in the pathway direction), FVA (each pathway reaction's flux range with the product held at 99% of that optimum), and flux sampling (the distribution of each reaction's flux over random-objective vertices of the same constrained space). The substrate from Discover replaces the medium's carbon source unless switched off below.
