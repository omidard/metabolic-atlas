Methods and limitations

Dataset

The atlas serves 35 genome-scale metabolic models (GEMs) of 4 bacterial species. Each model carries reactions with bounds and gene-protein-reaction rules, metabolites with formula and charge, gene nucleotide sequences, growth rate anchors per medium axis, and a biomass reaction.

Union map

The 3D map shows the union of all 35 models: 3,706 compartment-specific metabolites (of which 197 are currency carriers such as ATP, NAD(H), water and protons) and 5,055 reactions, partitioned into 14 pathway groups. Metabolites sit at fixed positions on three concentric shells by compartment: extracellular (radius 42), periplasm (26) and cytosol (13). Metabolites of other compartments keep their assigned positions. An edge runs from each substrate to each product of a reaction; edges touching a currency metabolite render dimmed. Node and edge colour encode the pathway group.

The 81 reactions carried by no individual GEM appear on the map and in search results with a presence of 0 of 35 strains; a pathway through them cannot be carried end-to-end by any strain.

Mode 1: pathway search by enzyme presence

The search enumerates simple paths from the substrate to the product over a directed metabolite graph. Each reaction contributes an edge from every substrate to every product; a reaction with a negative lower bound in at least one GEM also contributes the reverse edge. Currency metabolites are never used as intermediate nodes, so a pathway cannot pass through ATP, NAD(H), water or similar carriers; a currency metabolite may still be the chosen substrate or product. Compartment forms of a metabolite (_e, _p, _c) are distinct nodes joined by transport reactions.

Pathways are ranked by length, then by carrier count. Every result states whether the enumeration completed or stopped at a cap; a stopped search can miss pathways, and an empty result from a stopped search is not evidence of absence.

Strain presence means the strain's GEM contains at least one reaction for every step of the pathway. Presence does not check that the strain's own bounds permit each step in the required direction, does not check cofactor or redox balance, carbon conservation, thermodynamic feasibility, or flux feasibility; those checks belong to Mode 2. A pathway list is almost never complete: enumeration is bounded by the caps above and by the union network itself, which contains only reactions present in at least one of the 35 models.

GEM browser fields

Mass balanced counts reactions whose elemental and charge balance closes with the recorded formulas; balanceable additionally counts reactions that close after proton adjustment. GAM and NGAM are growth-associated and non-growth-associated ATP maintenance, in mmol ATP gDW-1 and mmol ATP gDW-1 h-1; the NGAM source is recorded per model. Growth rate anchors give the per-axis interval recorded in the model release, in h-1; they are model-derived values, not experimental measurements. Bound edits made in the reactions table are held in browser memory for the session; they are written to exports and will feed the phase 2 flux analyses. Reloading the page discards them.

Media

Nine predefined media ship with the data release, three axes per species where defined. The media editor and medium-conditioned analyses are part of Mode 2.

Release scope

This release covers Mode 1 search, the 3D union map and the GEM browser. Mode 2 (flux feasibility by pFBA, FVA and flux sampling), the media editor, knockout, MOMA and growth-coupling analyses, and SBML export are not part of this release; their controls appear only on the roadmap panel.

Provenance

Data release 2026-09-17. All computation runs in the browser; no query leaves the page. Cross-reference links resolve to KEGG, BiGG, RHEA, MetaNetX, ChEBI, SEED, HMDB and BioCyc.
