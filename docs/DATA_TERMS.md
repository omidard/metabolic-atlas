# Data terms and provenance

## What the atlas serves

Metabolic Atlas serves 35 genome-scale metabolic models (GEMs) of 4 bacterial
species (Parageobacillus thermoglucosidasius, Pseudomonas putida, Cupriavidus
necator, Eubacterium limosum). Each model carries reactions, metabolites, gene
rules, gene nucleotide sequences, growth anchors and a biomass reaction.

## Cross-reference data

Reactions and metabolites carry cross-reference identifiers to external
resources, and the browser links out to them. Cross-reference data from KEGG,
BiGG, MetaNetX, RHEA, ChEBI, SEED, HMDB and BioCyc is used under each resource's
own terms; identifiers resolve to the owning resource, which holds the
authoritative record. No third-party database is redistributed in bulk; the
atlas stores identifiers and links, and the model content itself.

Each resource sets its own reuse terms:

- KEGG: academic use under the KEGG licence; commercial use requires a licence.
- BiGG Models: released under a permissive academic licence.
- MetaNetX: CC BY 4.0.
- RHEA: CC BY 4.0.
- ChEBI: CC BY 4.0.
- ModelSEED / SEED: released for academic use.
- HMDB: free for academic and non-commercial use; commercial use requires a licence.
- BioCyc: subscription resource; identifiers link to the owning record.

Confirm the current terms at each resource before any downstream redistribution.
